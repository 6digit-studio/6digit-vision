# Verdict

## Sensing mode

Vision only. I read `TASK.md` and `render.sh`, looked at `reference.png` and
`candidate.png` with the image viewer, and edited HTML/CSS. No fact sheet was
read, no crops/zooms/pixel sampling/coordinate measuring/diff scripts were used;
all sizes, positions and colours were estimated by eye.

## What is missing

- **The two "agate" spheres (card 1, and the large one in card 4).** These are
  photographic organic pink/crimson filament textures with a black–navy
  bottom-left corner and a small blue glint. CSS radial-gradient blobs plus a
  light conic/ring overlay only approximate the colour layout and soft mottling;
  the crisp, brain-coral filament detail is absent, and the candidate reads
  slightly more diffuse and more magenta than the reference.
- **The "dawn" photographic gradients** (top of card 2, whole of card 4). The
  reference has a real photo-like horizon with subtle banding and warm-to-teal
  interplay; mine is a synthetic linear gradient, so the transition zones are
  smoother and a touch flatter, and card 4's mid-tones are a little lighter than
  the reference's deep navy.
- **Typeface.** The reference likely uses a custom grotesk; I substituted
  Helvetica Neue. Line breaks were forced manually to match, so line lengths and
  letter shapes differ by a few pixels.
- **The KODOR mark.** The chevron glyph next to the wordmark is a CSS
  `clip-path` approximation of a logo glyph I could only estimate by eye.
- **Small artefacts**: the faint vertical light streak under the card-1 sphere,
  the soft contact shadow of the card-3 pearl, and the fine grain/sharpness of
  the reference photography.
