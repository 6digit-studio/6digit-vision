## Sensing mode

Vision only — all judgments come from direct visual inspection of `reference.png` and my own rendered candidates (no eyes command, no fact sheet, no measurement dump).

## What is missing

- **Cyan edge-light is too weak and mis-shaped.** In the reference a thick, saturated teal band runs along the entire bottom edge of the keycap and up the right side to about mid-height, with a white-cyan hotspot near the bottom-right corner and a wide bloom onto the slot, the plate and the background. My version only lights a thin arc from the bottom-left corner to the bottom-right corner; the glow spills too diffusely and desaturates toward white.
- **White specular rim is too thick/bright and wraps too far.** The reference has a delicate, soft 1–1.5 px highlight that lives almost exclusively on the left edge and fades out well before the top edge and the bottom-left corner; mine is a ~3.5 px ring that continues around the top and lower-left, making the keycap read as glass.
- **Keycap/plate shading is flatter and lighter than the reference.** The reference face is darker over the lower-left and lower-right, with a soft, broad sheen just inside the upper-left edge and a distinct dark slot (near-black) between face and plate at the bottom/right; my face keeps a more uniform mid-grey and the slot is a soft gradient rather than a deep slot. The plate's beveled lip is also too even, missing the brighter outer lip at top-left and the black under-lip at the bottom.
- **Dot-matrix icon is under-luminous.** The reference dots are larger visually, nearly pure white, with a strong tight bloom and a cyan tint on the lower dots of the outermost wave. Mine are smaller, dimmer, and evenly spaced around the arcs (the reference arcs break into clustered groups), so the icon reads thinner and less "neon".
- **Background/ambient light is off.** The reference background is a fairly even dark blue-grey with a diffuse teal haze around the lower-right of the button and virtually no highlight halo around the top-left; mine has a noticeable light halo around the upper-left of the plate, slightly coarse grain, and a weaker bottom-right haze.

## Self-estimate

5 — The layout, keycap silhouette, dot icon structure and glow placement are recognizable, but the signature teal edge-light, the rim highlights and the tonal rendering of the plate/face differ clearly enough that it reads as a CSS approximation rather than the reference render.
