3. **Card shadow / backdrop falloff.** The reference's background darkens in a wider, gentler halo around the black card; mine shows a slightly tighter shadow ring at the card's left and right flanks and a marginally flatter mid-gray behind the lower half.
4. **Bar's pale right end.** The reference's cyan-to-white transition at x≈248–258 is a smooth bloom that merges into the plane; mine still reads as a distinct pale rounded tip just before the wing.
5. **Fine typography.** The 9:59 PM / 12:59 AM times and the status line are within ~1–2 px of the reference's baselines and 1–3 px of its ink width, but cap heights and inter-letter rhythm (notably "San Francisco") still differ slightly because of the reference's blur and likely SF-Pro optical-size differences.
