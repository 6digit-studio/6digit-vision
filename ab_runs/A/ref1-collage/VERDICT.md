# Verdict

## Sensing mode

Vision only — every judgement came from viewing `reference.png` and my own `candidate.png` (plus self-made zoom crops and ruler grids); no `eyes` command, fact sheet, measurement dump or external analysis was used.

## What is missing

1. **Orb interiors.** The reference's glass spheres contain a richly detailed 3-D coral/brain of white-pink ridges, bright speculars and dark red valleys; my procedural SVG version (displaced rings + generated lobes + noise) reads as a softer, plumper blob with far less filigree and no true chromatic aberration inside the glass.
2. **Card 4's rainbow fringe.** Mine is a clean, fairly thick conic ring at the rim; the original's aberration is thinner, irregular and broken, fading in and out around the circumference, and the orb's lower half in the reference is a deeper navy with dark red arcs rather than my lighter blue pool.
3. **Typeface substitution.** The design uses a geometric sans that is slightly narrower than macOS SF Pro, so headline/body widths and the "KODOR" wordmark letterforms (notably the R, D and O) do not match exactly, even though sizes, tracking and line breaks were tuned to the measured widths.
4. **Small tonal/detail differences in the flat cards.** My card 1 gradient/shadow is slightly cleaner and brighter than the reference, the card 2 "dawn" gradient lacks the original's faint grain/vignette, and the card 3 blue sphere is a little lighter and more metallic with a weaker under-ball reflection.
5. **Orb placement/edge detail.** The orbs' outer silhouettes, rim highlights and drop shadows are close, but the reference has a bright white top-left rim arc and small blue-white glass reflections that mine only approximates.

## Self-estimate

7 — layout, card geometry, type scale, logos, all copy, and the broad colour fields line up closely at 1520×794, but the hand-built CSS/SVG orbs remain visibly less detailed and less glassy than the original 3-D renders, which are the page's focal point.
